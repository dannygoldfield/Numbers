## Follow-up disposition — decision chronology

Q01 is now a confirmed candidate denomination decision: indivisible rana with exact integer accounting. This is an observable amount-validity rule, not a behavior-neutral implementation shortcut. It remains a Prototype 1 rule and does not establish a sats conversion or production denomination.

At the initial follow-up, the user’s agreement with the prior Q02–Q04 entries did not choose their economic alternatives, because each entry explicitly supplied no recommendation. The later C45/C46 decisions and S14/S15 below resolve the hold and release rules.

## Source conflicts resolved by existing authority, not new product choices

1. **Sequence creation and time.** `core/TRANSITION-INVARIANTS.md` TI-09 says time must never create an auction. Higher `core/INVARIANTS.md` I-01 explicitly requires N+1 creation at the next evaluation after finalization and the gap. Retain that narrow, record-gated creation and never time-open Scheduled. No question is needed.
2. **Extension configuration capture.** `config/CONFIG-REFERENCE.md` §6 says max_extensions fixes at opening; higher `IMPLEMENTATION-SLICE-01.md` §5 captures it in AuctionRecord with other timing/admission parameters. Retain earlier capture and prohibit later reinterpretation.
3. **Deferred intent atomicity.** `core/STATE-MACHINE.md` §5.8 allows an immediate later append, while higher `core/STATE-MACHINE-TABLE.md` I requires an atomic triple. The table controls old Demo 1. Prototype 1 removes the inscription requirement by S01, not by exploiting the looser wording.
4. **Local settlement versus chain expiry.** `core/STATE-MACHINE.md` §3.4 uses chain-style exit language. Higher `core/STATE-MACHINE-TABLE.md` I explicitly supplies local commands without deadline preconditions. Retain those local triggers; do not silently add expiry on restart.
5. **Inscription authority versus broad ambiguity fields.** `data/DATA-MODEL.md` §15 enumerates auction/settlement/system authority labels, but higher `core/AUTHORITY-CONSUMPTION.md` §3 defines exactly inscription authority. The broad enum grants no additional authority. All such external ambiguity machinery is excluded from Prototype 1 by S01/S09.
